Our Story

From HVAC Business Owner to Local SEO Expert

Local Boost Labs wasn't born in a boardroom—it was born in the field, servicing AC units in the South Carolina heat.

In 2015, our founder Marcus Thompson owned and operated a successful HVAC company in Lancaster, SC. Like many contractors, he struggled to get found online despite providing excellent service. He watched competitors with inferior work dominate Google while his phone stayed silent.

Frustrated but determined, Marcus dove deep into the world of Local SEO. He spent nights studying Google's algorithms, testing strategies on his own business, and learning what actually moves the needle for local service companies. Within 18 months, his HVAC company went from page 3 to the #1 position in the Map Pack—and his phone started ringing off the hook.

Other HVAC contractors noticed. They started asking Marcus for help. In 2019, he made a pivotal decision: sell his HVAC company and dedicate himself fully to helping other contractors achieve the same success. Local Boost Labs was born.

"I've walked in your boots. I know what it's like to wait by the phone hoping for it to ring. That's why I built this agency—to make sure no great HVAC contractor stays invisible online." — Marcus Thompson, Founder & CEO

Our Focus

Why We Work Exclusively with HVAC Companies

While other agencies spread themselves thin across dozens of industries, we made a strategic decision: become the absolute best at one thing rather than mediocre at many.

The HVAC industry has unique challenges that generalist agencies simply don't understand:

  • Seasonal demand patterns — AC peaks in summer, heating in winter
  • Emergency service keywords — "AC repair near me" at 2 AM
  • Trust signals unique to home services — licensing, insurance, reviews
  • Service area targeting — multiple cities without multiple locations
  • High-intent local searches — people need help NOW

By focusing exclusively on HVAC, we've developed proprietary strategies, templates, and systems specifically designed for heating and cooling contractors. This specialization means better results for you.
