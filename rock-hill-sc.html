About Rock Hill, SC

Rock Hill is the largest city in York County and the fifth-largest city in South Carolina. Located along I-77 approximately 25 miles south of Charlotte, Rock Hill serves as a regional hub for commerce, education, and healthcare.

The city is home to Winthrop University, a major employer and economic driver. Rock Hill's economy includes manufacturing, healthcare (Piedmont Medical Center), retail, and a growing tech sector. The city has invested heavily in downtown revitalization, including the Knowledge Park development and various urban renewal projects.

Rock Hill's combination of established neighborhoods, new development, commercial properties, and diverse demographics creates broad demand for HVAC services. Companies that establish strong local search visibility can capture significant market share in this important South Carolina market.
