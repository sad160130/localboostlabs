Your Google Business Profile for Contractors, Done Right

When a homeowner searches "contractor near me," the first thing they see is the map and the top three listings—the Map Pack. That's where the calls go. If your profile is incomplete, miscategorized, or missing photos, you're invisible at the exact moment someone's ready to hire.

We optimize your Google Business Profile for contractors so the right homeowners in your area find you first. It's one of the highest-leverage things you can do for local visibility—and it works hand in hand with your website and local landing pages.

What We Optimize on Your Profile

A profile that ranks isn't an accident. It's the result of getting every detail right and keeping it current.

Categories

Your primary and secondary categories tell Google exactly what you do. Pick the wrong ones and you don't show up for the searches that matter. We choose the categories that match how homeowners actually search for your trade.

Services

We list out every service you offer with clear descriptions, so your profile shows up for specific jobs—not just your trade in general.

Service Areas

We define the cities and neighborhoods you cover so you appear for searches across your whole service area, not just the town your office sits in.

Photos

Profiles with real, quality photos of your work and team get more clicks and calls. We help you put your best jobs front and center.

Reviews Strategy

We help you set up a simple, repeatable way to ask happy customers for reviews and respond to the ones you get—all real, all earned.

NAP Consistency

Your name, address, and phone number need to match everywhere online. We make sure your profile lines up with your website and listings so Google trusts your business.

Why the Map Pack Wins You Jobs

The Map Pack—those top three local results with the map—sits above almost everything else on the page. For a contractor, that placement is the difference between a phone that rings and one that doesn't.

Local search is where homeowners go to hire. 46% of all Google searches have local intent (Google/Ipsos)—people looking for a service near them, ready to act. An optimized profile is how you claim your spot when they search.

  • Top-of-page visibility — the Map Pack appears before regular results.
  • Tap to call — homeowners can call you straight from the listing.
  • Directions and hours — everything they need to choose you, in one place.
  • Reviews on display — your reputation works for you 24/7.
