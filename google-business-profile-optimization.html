Why Google Business Profile Matters for HVAC

Your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is often the first thing potential customers see when searching for HVAC services. It appears prominently in the Map Pack—the top 3 local results that get 44% of all clicks.

For HVAC companies, an optimized GBP can mean the difference between getting the call or losing it to a competitor. When someone's AC breaks down, they don't scroll through pages of results—they call one of the top 3 businesses they see.
