Why These Services Work Together

Homeowners hire the contractor they can find and trust first. Local search is where that decision happens—and the numbers back it up.

  • 46% of all Google searches have local intent. (Source: Google/Ipsos)
  • 88% of local mobile searches lead to a call or visit within 24 hours. (Source: BrightLocal)
  • 44% of clicks go to the top 3 results in Google's Map Pack. (Source: BrightLocal)

Your website is the foundation. Google Business Profile, local landing pages, and reviews are how you climb the rankings and get in front of more of those ready-to-hire homeowners.
