Contractor Review Management That's Honest and Effective

Your reputation is your most valuable asset. Homeowners are letting you into their home—and before they call, they read your reviews. A page full of recent 5-star reviews wins the job. A thin or stale one sends them to a competitor.

We help you earn real reviews from real customers and respond to them the right way. No fake reviews, ever—just a simple system that turns the happy customers you already have into the proof future customers need. It works hand in hand with your Google Business Profile and website.

Why Reviews Matter for Local Search & Trust

Reviews do double duty. They convince homeowners you're the right call—and they're one of the signals Google uses to decide who shows up in local search. More recent, genuine reviews mean more trust and more visibility.

Local search is where these decisions happen. 46% of all Google searches have local intent (Google/Ipsos), and when those searchers land on the map, your star rating and review count are right there shaping their choice. Reviews are how you win that glance.

  • Trust at a glance — recent reviews tell homeowners you do the job right.
  • Local ranking signal — reviews help you show up in the Map Pack.
  • Free, ongoing proof — your best customers sell for you 24/7.
  • An edge over competitors — the contractor with better reviews usually gets the call.

Responding to Reviews the Right Way

Getting reviews is only half of it. How you respond says just as much to the next homeowner reading them.

A quick thank-you on a great review shows you value your customers. A calm, professional reply to a tough one shows you stand behind your work—and that's often more persuasive than a perfect rating. We help you handle both so your reputation works for you, not against you.

  • Thank your fans — a short reply turns a happy customer into a loyal one.
  • Stay professional — we help you answer criticism calmly and constructively.
  • Show you care — future customers judge you by how you respond.
  • Protect your name — the right response keeps one bad day from defining you.
